Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from homeowners across the Charlotte area.

Do you charge extra to travel to Charlotte or surrounding cities?

No travel surcharge for any location within the Charlotte metro including Concord, Harrisburg, Huntersville, Mooresville, Mint Hill, and Matthews. We may add a small fuel surcharge for locations beyond 35 miles from Harrisburg — we'll let you know upfront.

How quickly can you get to my home?

We typically schedule estimates within 24–48 hours and installations within 2–3 business days of the estimate. For urgent situations, call us directly — we often have same-day availability.

Can you add ethernet to a finished home without tearing up walls?

Yes — that's our specialty. We use wall-fishing techniques and low-profile keystone plates to route cable through finished walls with minimal disruption. Most homeowners are surprised at how clean the result looks.

What's the difference between hiring you vs. an internet provider's tech?

ISP technicians handle the modem and the line coming into your home. They don't run ethernet to rooms, install access points, or do structured cabling. That's our specialty — we pick up where the ISP tech leaves off.

Do you work on new construction as well as existing homes?

Yes — we handle both rough-in wiring for new builds and retrofit installs in finished homes. For new construction, it's best to have us in before drywall to minimize cost and maximize flexibility.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. We warranty our workmanship for 1 year. If a termination fails or a run tests below spec, we come back and fix it at no charge.
