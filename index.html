Common Questions

Quick answers before you call or book.

How much does Cat6 wiring cost?

Most single-room ethernet drops run $150–$300 depending on run length, wall material, and access. Whole-home projects vary by size and number of drops. You'll always get a flat-rate quote before any work starts — no surprises.

How fast can you schedule?

Most appointments happen within 2–3 business days. Urgent situations are often accommodated sooner — just call and ask. We keep schedule flexibility for same-week bookings.

Do you work in my area?

Cooper Electrical serves Harrisburg, Concord, Kannapolis, Huntersville, Davidson, Mooresville, and the greater Charlotte metro. Not sure if you're in range? Just call — we can usually make it work.

Will you patch holes in the drywall?

Wiring runs are done as cleanly as possible using existing pathways and low-profile wall plates. Minor drywall penetrations are typical for in-wall wiring. We'll discuss the routing plan with you beforehand so there are no surprises about where cuts are made.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. Every job comes with a personal satisfaction guarantee. If something isn't right with the work after we leave, we'll come back and make it right. No charge, no argument.

What's the difference between mesh WiFi and wired access points?

Mesh systems (like Eero or Orbi) rely on wireless backhaul between nodes, which eats half your bandwidth. Wired access points get a dedicated Cat6 connection to each unit — full speeds everywhere, no wireless interference, far more reliable. Cooper Electrical specializes in the wired approach.
