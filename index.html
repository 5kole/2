FAQ

Common Questions

How much does Cat6 wiring cost?

Most single-room ethernet drops run $150–$300 depending on the run length, wall material, and access. Whole-home projects vary by home size and number of drops. You'll always get a flat-rate quote before any work starts — no surprises.

How fast can you schedule?

Most appointments happen within 2–3 business days. Urgent situations are often accommodated sooner — just call and ask. Cole keeps schedule flexibility for same-week bookings.

Do you work in my area?

Cooper Electrical serves Harrisburg, Concord, Kannapolis, Huntersville, and the greater Charlotte metro area. Not sure if you're in range? Just call — Cole can usually make it work.

Will you patch holes in the drywall?

Wiring runs are done as cleanly as possible, using existing pathways and low-profile wall plates. Minor drywall penetrations are typical for in-wall wiring. Cole will discuss the routing plan with you beforehand so there are no surprises about where cuts are made.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. Every job comes with a personal satisfaction guarantee. If something isn't right with the work after Cole leaves, he'll come back and make it right. No charge, no argument.

What's the difference between a mesh WiFi system and wired access points?

Mesh systems (like Eero or Orbi) rely on wireless backhaul between nodes, which eats half your bandwidth. Wired access points get a dedicated Cat6 connection to each unit — full speeds everywhere, no wireless interference, and far more reliable. Cooper Electrical specializes in the wired approach.
