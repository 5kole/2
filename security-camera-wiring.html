Security Camera FAQ

Common questions from homeowners in the Harrisburg & Charlotte area.

Do you supply the cameras or just run the wire?

We primarily handle the wiring — the part that requires a licensed technician. Most customers source their own cameras and NVR (Reolink, Lorex, Hikvision are popular). If you need help selecting equipment, we're happy to advise during the estimate.

What's the difference between PoE and coax (BNC) cameras?

PoE (Power over Ethernet) cameras use a single Cat5e/Cat6 cable for both power and video — they're the modern standard and offer the best image quality. BNC/coax systems are older but still common. We install both; we'll ask what system you have or are buying before quoting.

Can you add cameras to my existing WiFi system?

We can run ethernet to any location where you want a hardwired PoE camera, effectively upgrading your setup. If your existing cameras are wireless-only, you'd need to replace them with PoE models — we can discuss this during the estimate.

How many cameras can you install in one visit?

A 4-camera exterior install typically takes 4–6 hours. An 8-camera whole-home system usually runs a full day. We'll give you a time estimate with your flat-rate quote so you can plan accordingly.

Will you patch holes or touch up after running cable?

We minimize penetrations and use low-profile entry points wherever possible. We are not a drywall or painting contractor — if interior patching is needed beyond our standard cleanup, we'll note it in the quote upfront.

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve Harrisburg, Concord, Kannapolis, Huntersville, Mooresville, and the greater Charlotte, NC metro area. Call us if you're not sure — we may be able to accommodate.
