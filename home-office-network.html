Home Office Network FAQ

Quick answers before you call.

My router is two floors away — can you still run ethernet to my office?

Yes, that's one of the most common jobs we do. We route the cable through interior walls, using existing chases where possible, to keep the run hidden. We'll tell you the routing plan and exact price before we start.

How much does a home office ethernet drop cost?

A single ethernet drop typically ranges from $149–$279 depending on run length, number of floors, and wall type (drywall vs. brick vs. exterior walls). We always give you a flat-rate quote before touching anything.

Will the cable be visible on the wall?

No — we wall-fish the cable so only the wall plate is visible at each end. In older homes or specific situations where we can't fish a wall, we'll discuss a raceway option before we start.

Can you add a new electrical outlet in my office at the same time?

Yes. Cole is a licensed electrician, so we can add an outlet, a USB charging outlet, or a dedicated circuit in the same visit. We'll bundle it into one flat-rate quote.

Do I need to buy any hardware beforehand?

No — we supply the Cat6 cable, wall plates, and keystones. If you want us to install a specific access point or switch you've already purchased, that works too. Just let us know ahead of time.

How soon can you schedule the installation?

Most jobs are scheduled within 2–3 business days. Call us in the morning and there's a good chance we can get you on the schedule for the same week.
