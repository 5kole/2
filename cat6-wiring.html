Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common Cat6 questions we hear.

What's the difference between Cat5e and Cat6?

Cat6 supports 10-Gigabit speeds up to 55 meters and has stricter crosstalk specs than Cat5e. For most homes, Cat5e would work fine, but Cat6 is the same price from us and future-proofs your install for 10+ years.

Will you patch the drywall if you have to cut holes?

We use low-profile wall plates and fish walls without cutting large holes. In most finished homes, we create only the outlet hole (about the size of an outlet box). We do not do drywall finishing — if patching is needed, we'll note it in the quote upfront.

How long does a typical installation take?

A single drop takes 1–2 hours. A 3-drop package usually takes 3–4 hours. Whole-home installs are typically 1–2 days depending on the number of runs and house size.

Do I need to be home during the installation?

Yes — we need access to your home and need you to confirm outlet locations and the routing path before we start fishing walls. Someone 18+ should be on site.

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve Harrisburg, Concord, Kannapolis, Huntersville, Mooresville, and the greater Charlotte, NC metro area. Call us if you're not sure — we may be able to accommodate.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. We warranty our workmanship for 1 year. If a termination fails or a run tests below spec, we fix it at no charge.
